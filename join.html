Lifestyle & Health

A measured year, kept in company.

Most longevity plans end in a drawer: one test, one report, and life carries on as before. We built the year the other way round.

I · Your baseline

The year begins at VITAE, with a precise reading of how you breathe: the mechanics of your lungs, how your body handles carbon dioxide, and your lung age. You receive it as a personal report, written to be understood.

II · Your physician

A private consultation with a longevity physician specialised in health optimisation, who reads your results with you, alongside your blood work and diagnostics, and sets one clear goal for your year. Independent, licensed, and on your side.

III · Your year

The House accompanies you through it. Every six months your physician reviews your progress, you are measured again, and the goal is set anew, so you can see what is changing rather than guess.

IV · Your circle

You do not do it alone. You breathe, move, eat and recover with people walking the same road, at the table, on the water and in the rooms we open between gatherings. This is how measuring your health stops being an event and becomes a habit.

Why it works: across 148 studies and more than 300,000 people, those with strong social ties were 50% more likely to survive over the period studied (Holt-Lunstad et al., PLOS Medicine, 2010). Health is easier to keep when it is shared.

Also kept close

Seasonal Issues. The private digital clubhouse. A shared House Desk for places, gatherings and selected reservations. Partner privileges and introductions when there is a genuine reason for two people to meet.

The Atlas

The Atlas carries the House between gatherings — a private map bringing together places we trust and the members moving between them. See who will be in Zürich, Dubai or Milan the same week, share a city before you arrive, and let a coffee, dinner or introduction happen because your paths crossed. Each member chooses the city, dates and visibility of what they share; precise and live locations are never displayed.
