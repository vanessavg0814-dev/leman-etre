The Dolce Vita

A private Italian escape on the Sorrento coast — three nights of nature, flavour and the Mediterranean lifestyle.
Ten places only, by invitation.

Reserve your place →

There is a way of living the Italians never had to learn — unhurried, generous, lit by long lunches and the sea. The House goes to find it, on the coast between Sorrento and Amalfi, where the days are measured in flavour and good company.

A private villa in the Mediterranean greenery. Peaceful mornings, a table that never rushes, and a small group gathered for the pleasure of sharing the time.

Slow down. Savour more.

A journey through nature, culture, flavour and the Italian art of living: the craft of the kitchen, a day at sea to Capri and Positano, the colour of the Amalfi Coast — and the quiet luxury of nowhere to be.

Hosted and curated by LÉMAN ÊTRE, in the register all gatherings of the House keep: beautiful, considered, and entirely among friends.

The programme

Four days, in the Italian key

Mornings belong to the garden and the table, days to the coast and the sea, evenings to long dinners that are the real schedule of the week.

Day One · Friday 1 October

Arrive & settle

Afternoon
Arrive at the private villa above the Sorrento coast — Mediterranean greenery, a first swim, and the slow exhale of the south.
Evening
A welcome dinner in the garden, prepared with the day's market and the warmth of Italian hospitality.
Day Two · Saturday 2 October

Slow down, savour more

Morning
A gentle start in the gardens, breakfast unhurried on the terrace.
Afternoon
The art of Italian cooking, hands in the dough — fresh ingredients, traditional recipes, moments worth sharing.
Evening
An optional limoncello tasting, then private dining under the vines.
Day Three · Sunday 3 October

A day at sea

Day
A private yacht along the coast to Capri and Positano — swimming in crystal-clear water, a leisurely lunch by the sea.
Golden hour
Back along the Amalfi coast as the light turns, the villages glowing on the cliffs.
Evening
A long table, the good bottles, the sea still in everyone's hair.
Day Four · Monday 4 October

The coast & farewell

Morning
A last Mediterranean morning — the colourful villages and timeless scenery of the Amalfi Coast.
Midday
Unhurried departures, carrying the pace of the place home with you.
Optional
By arrangement, a private journey through ancient Pompeii and the extraordinary landscapes of Mount Vesuvius.
